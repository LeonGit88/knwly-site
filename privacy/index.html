WEBSITE & ENQUIRIES

Your enquiry.
Your control.

How this website handles the details you enter and the services you choose to use.

The project form

The enquiry builder prepares a summary inside your browser. It does not submit your details to KNWLY, store them in a database or save them in browser storage.

You choose whether to copy the enquiry or open an email draft. The details are shared with KNWLY when you send the email. Refreshing or leaving the page clears the prepared summary.

Email & calls

Email enquiries go to knwly.dev@gmail.com. KNWLY uses the information you send to discuss and respond to your project. Avoid including passwords, access tokens or sensitive customer data in an initial enquiry.

Booking a call takes you to Calendly. That service handles the information you enter under its own privacy terms. Your email service also applies its own terms when you send an enquiry.

Site services

This website does not include marketing trackers or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider handles the requests needed to serve the website. The private review version requires the owner’s sign-in through its hosting service.

Fonts are served by this website rather than loaded from an external font service. Animations follow your device’s reduced motion setting.

Contact

For questions about an enquiry you have sent, or to ask about access, correction or deletion of that information, email KNWLY at knwly.dev@gmail.com.
