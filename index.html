BEFORE WE START

Clear answers.
Before we build.

What we take on, how delivery works and what you need to get started.

Can you take on the entire project?+

Yes. Complete platform development is our core offering: customer applications, backend services, integrations, admin tools and deployment. We can also develop a specific system or work alongside your existing team.

Can you improve an existing platform?+

Yes. We review the product, its architecture and the constraints around it before recommending the work. The scope might be an integration, an operational tool, a product extension or a wider rebuild.

How are scope, cost and timing agreed?+

We review requirements, existing systems and dependencies before agreeing a delivery plan. We define milestones, review points and acceptance criteria together, and identify anything that needs investigation before committing to an estimate.

What happens at handover?+

We agree documentation, access, deployment responsibilities and ongoing support as part of the engagement. The plan sets out what your team will take over and which responsibilities remain with KNWLY.

What do I need for the first conversation?+

An explanation of your business, what you want to build or improve, and any existing product or documentation. A rough timeline and budget help, but you do not need a finished technical specification.

Do you also build business software?+

Yes. Alongside complete platforms, we build custom CRMs, internal portals, workflow automation and integrations around the way a business operates.
