Project information

Service descriptions explain the kinds of software KNWLY builds. Project examples describe previous scope. They do not promise the same features, costs, timelines or results for a new engagement.

“Experience across 30+ projects” describes project experience. The figure does not represent 30 clients. Platform diagrams illustrate software structure rather than live client systems.

Enquiries and scope

Using the enquiry builder or booking a conversation does not create a development agreement. Budget selections are conversation guides, not quotations. Deliverables, costs, timelines and support responsibilities are agreed separately for each engagement.

The enquiry builder prepares a summary for you to review and send through your email service. See our privacy information for how it works.

External services

Links to booking and social services take you to third party websites. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you use them.
